## [v0.1.0] - 2026-07-13 ([`7504317`](https://github.com/plexusone/workloadguard/commit/7504317))

### Highlights

- macOS system health daemon that detects and mitigates runaway processes
- Dual-trigger monitoring: periodic checks plus immediate checks when load exceeds threshold
- Cedar policy engine for formal policy evaluation with composable rules
- Prometheus metrics for full observability
